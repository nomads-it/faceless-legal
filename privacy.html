Privacy Policy — Faceless Studio

Last updated: 16 August 2026

What this app is

Faceless Studio is a personal, self-hosted tool that generates short videos and, at the owner's request, uploads them to the owner's own social media accounts. It runs on the owner's computer. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What data is collected

The app stores, on the owner's own computer only:

  • Access and refresh tokens for accounts the owner explicitly connects (YouTube, TikTok), used solely to upload the owner's own videos.
  • The connected account's display name and account identifier, shown in the interface so the owner can tell accounts apart.
  • Videos the app generates, and the settings used to generate them.

No data about any other person is collected. No analytics, advertising, tracking or profiling of any kind is performed.

How data is used and shared

Tokens are used only to call the platform APIs the owner connected, in order to upload the owner's videos. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data never leaves the owner's machine except in those direct API calls.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app or revokes access at the platform, whichever comes first; disconnecting deletes the stored token immediately. Generated videos remain on the owner's disk until deleted in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the repository owner via the contact address on the account used to register this application.
